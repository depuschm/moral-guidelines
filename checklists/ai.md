## Fundamental (9–10)

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `AI-2` (8) Use AI only where it is necessary and proportionate to a legitimate aim, and assess the risks to people's rights, safety and the environment before you deploy it and whenever it changes. ([specific/ai](../specific/ai/README.md)) *Supported by: Algorithmic Impact Assessment tool.*
- [ ] `AI-3` (8) Keep people in control of AI that makes or shapes decisions about people: someone competent must be able to understand its output, ignore or override it, and stop the system. ([specific/ai](../specific/ai/README.md))
- [ ] `AI-4` (8) Make clear who is accountable for each AI system, keep records so that its outputs can be traced and audited, and give people a way to challenge its decisions and get redress. ([specific/ai](../specific/ai/README.md)) *Supported by: Algorithmic Impact Assessment tool.*
- [ ] `AI-6` (8) Test AI systems for harmful bias before you deploy them and while they are in use, across the groups of people they affect, and fix the system or stop using it if it treats people unfairly. - [ ] `AI-7` (8) Tell people when they are interacting with an AI system rather than a person, and label deepfakes and other AI-generated content, unless it is obvious. ([specific/ai](../specific/ai/README.md))
- [ ] `AI-11` (8) Protect the privacy of the people whose data is used to train, test or run an AI system, throughout its life cycle. ([specific/ai](../specific/ai/README.md)) *Supported by: Datasheets for Datasets.*
- [ ] `AI-5` (7) Train the people who use or oversee AI to understand what it can and can't do, and guard against automation bias, the tendency to rely too much on a system's output. ([specific/ai](../specific/ai/README.md))
