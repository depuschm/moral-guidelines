# Security research checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

This file is generated by `python3 scripts/rank.py fix` from the guidelines. Don't edit it by hand.

## Fundamental (9–10)

- [ ] `SECRES-1` (9) Test only systems you own, have permission to test, or that the owner has opened to research in a published vulnerability disclosure policy, and stay within its scope and terms. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md)) *Supported by: VDP with Coordinated Disclosure Window.*
- [ ] `SECRES-3` (9) If a vulnerability gives you access to personal or confidential data, access only the minimum needed to prove it, stop testing and report it straight away; use only test accounts you own or have permission to use. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md))
- [ ] `SECRES-10` (9) Never use a vulnerability to threaten its owner or demand payment, and don't make your report or your silence depend on a reward. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md))

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `SECRES-2` (8) Do only what you need to show that a vulnerability exists: don't disrupt services, change or destroy data, or harm the experience of users. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md))
- [ ] `SECRES-5` (8) Report a vulnerability first to the vendor or owner through its official channel, or through a coordinator such as a national CERT if you can't reach it or it doesn't respond. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md)) *Supported by: security.txt, RFC 9116.*
- [ ] `SECRES-6` (8) Agree a disclosure date with the vendor, give it a reasonable time to fix the problem before you publish, and publish once a fix is available or the agreed deadline has passed. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md)) *Supported by: VDP with Coordinated Disclosure Window, Vulnerability Disclosure Policy Templates.*
- [ ] `SECRES-7` (8) Move faster when a vulnerability is being exploited or threatens critical infrastructure, health or safety: involve a coordinator, and shorten the disclosure timeline so that people can protect themselves. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md))
- [ ] `SECRES-8` (7) When you publish, give users and defenders enough detail to judge their risk and protect themselves, and weigh what extra detail, such as a working exploit, would give attackers. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md))
- [ ] `SECRES-11` (7) If you receive vulnerability reports, make reporting easy, presume the reporter acts in good faith, respond promptly, keep them informed and don't take legal action against good-faith research. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md)) *Supported by: security.txt, RFC 9116, Vulnerability Disclosure Policy Templates.*
- [ ] `SECRES-4` (6) Get ethics review for research that collects data about people or could affect systems at scale, such as internet-wide measurements, and weigh the risks to third parties who never agreed to take part. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md))
- [ ] `SECRES-9` (6) When a vulnerability affects several vendors or a shared component, coordinate with all of them, directly or through a coordinator, so that users of every affected product can be protected. ([specific/security](../specific/security/README.md))
