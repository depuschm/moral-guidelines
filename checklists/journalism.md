# Journalism checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `JOUR-3` (8) Correct significant errors promptly and with due prominence, say what was wrong, and apologise where appropriate. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md)) *Supported by: Trust Indicators.*
- [ ] `JOUR-4` (8) Give the people and organisations you criticise or accuse of wrongdoing a fair opportunity to respond before you publish. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md))
- [ ] `JOUR-7` (8) Avoid conflicts of interest: refuse gifts, favours, fees, free travel and special treatment that could influence your coverage, and disclose the interests you can't avoid to your editors and, where relevant, to your audience. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md))
- [ ] `JOUR-8` (8) Weigh the public's need for information against the harm that publishing may cause, and take particular care with victims of crime, bereaved people and others who are vulnerable or inexperienced with the media. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md))
- [ ] `JOUR-10` (8) Intrude on someone's privacy only when a clear public interest justifies it, such as exposing crime or serious wrongdoing, protecting health and safety, or stopping the public being misled, and be ready to explain how you reached that decision at the time. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md))
- [ ] `JOUR-11` (8) Use deception, hidden cameras or recorders, or undercover methods only as a last resort: when there is evidence of something the public needs to know and you can't get it openly. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md))
- [ ] `JOUR-12` (8) Report suicide in ways that don't increase the risk of further suicides: don't describe the method or name the location, don't use sensational headlines, and tell readers where they can find help. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md))
- [ ] `JOUR-5` (7) Keep news, opinion and advertising clearly apart: label opinion and analysis as such, and label sponsored content and partnerships prominently. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md)) *Supported by: Trust Indicators.*
- [ ] `JOUR-13` (7) Mention a person's race, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or health only when it is relevant to the story, and avoid stereotypes and prejudicial references. ([specific/journalism](../specific/journalism/README.md))
