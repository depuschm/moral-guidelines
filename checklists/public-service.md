# Public service checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

## Fundamental (9–10)

- [ ] `PUB-1` (9) Act solely in the public interest: never use your office, its powers, public money, property or official information for your own benefit or that of your family, friends, party or business associates. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md))
- [ ] `PUB-2` (9) Decide impartially, on the merits and the best evidence: treat everyone fairly, give no undue preference to any person or group, and never discriminate. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md))
- [ ] `PUB-5` (9) Declare your private interests, and deal with any conflict of interest before it affects your work: watch for conflicts, disclose them as soon as you are aware of them, and step back from decisions where your interests, or those of your family or associates, could influence you or appear to. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md)) *Supported by: OECD Guidelines for Managing Conflict of Interest in the Public Service.*

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `PUB-3` (8) Appoint and promote people, and award public contracts, on merit through fair and open competition with objective criteria set in advance, and keep out favouritism and nepotism. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md))
- [ ] `PUB-6` (8) Refuse gifts, hospitality and favours that could influence your judgement, or reasonably look as if they could, and report any bribe that is offered to you. - [ ] `PUB-8` (8) Be truthful: never give ministers, the legislature or the public information you know or believe to be false or misleading, and don't withhold information that should properly be released. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md))
- [ ] `PUB-9` (8) Be open about decisions and the reasons for them: publish information about how your body works and decides, and answer requests for information on time; withhold information only for clear and lawful reasons. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md))
- [ ] `PUB-10` (8) Accept scrutiny: answer for your decisions to the public and to the bodies that oversee you, such as audit institutions, ombudsmen, regulators and courts, and respond properly to their findings. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md))
- [ ] `PUB-11` (8) Report corruption and other wrongdoing through the proper channels, and make sure that colleagues who report in good faith and on reasonable grounds suffer no reprisal. - [ ] `PUB-4` (7) If you are a non-elected official, serve the lawful government of the day impartially, whatever its politics and your own, and don't let your office be used for party political purposes. ([specific/public-service](../specific/public-service/README.md))
- [ ] `PUB-7` (7) Respect cooling-off periods when you leave public office: for the period the rules set, don't act or lobby for anyone on matters you dealt with in office, and never use confidential information from your former job.
