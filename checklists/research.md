# Research with people and animals checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

This file is generated by `python3 scripts/rank.py fix` from the guidelines. Don't edit it by hand.

## Fundamental (9–10)

- [ ] `RES-1` (10) Get each participant's free and informed consent before they take part, and respect their right to refuse or to withdraw at any time without penalty. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))
- [ ] `RES-2` (9) Have the research approved by an independent research ethics committee before it starts, and keep the committee informed until it ends. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))
- [ ] `RES-3` (9) Do research only when its expected value outweighs the risks and burdens to participants, keep those risks as low as possible, and stop or change the study if the balance turns. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))
- [ ] `RES-4` (9) Choose participants for scientific reasons, not because they are easy to recruit, and give people in vulnerable situations specific protections instead of excluding them by default. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))
- [ ] `RES-7` (9) Never fabricate, falsify or plagiarise, whether you are proposing, doing, reviewing or reporting research. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `RES-5` (8) Protect participants' privacy and the confidentiality of their data and samples, and reuse them only with consent or with a research ethics committee's approval. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))
- [ ] `RES-8` (8) Register each clinical trial and other medical study with people in a public registry before the first participant enrols, and make all the results public, including negative and inconclusive ones. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md)) *Supported by: WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform.*
- [ ] `RES-10` (8) Disclose financial and other interests that could affect, or seem to affect, your research, in proposals, ethics applications, publications, peer reviews and public statements. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md)) *Supported by: ICMJE Disclosure of Interest form.*
- [ ] `RES-12` (8) Use animals only when no method without animals can answer the research question, and apply the 3Rs: replace animals wherever possible, reduce their number to what a sound design needs, and refine housing, care and procedures to minimise pain, suffering and distress. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md)) *Supported by: ARRIVE guidelines 2.0.*
- [ ] `RES-6` (7) Make sure that participants harmed by the research get free treatment and fair compensation, and arrange before a clinical trial starts how participants who still need a treatment shown to be beneficial will get it afterwards. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))
- [ ] `RES-9` (7) List as authors everyone who meets the authorship criteria, and only them, and acknowledge everyone else who contributed. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md)) *Supported by: CRediT, Contributor Role Taxonomy.*
- [ ] `RES-11` (7) Report suspected research misconduct to the body responsible for investigating it, and handle allegations fairly: protect whistleblowers who act in good faith, and presume the accused innocent until proven otherwise. ([specific/research](../specific/research/README.md))
