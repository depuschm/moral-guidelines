# Business and human rights checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

## Fundamental (9–10)

- [ ] `BIZ-1` (9) Respect human rights wherever you operate: avoid causing or contributing to harm to people's rights through your own activities, and seek to prevent or reduce harm linked to your operations, products or services through your business relationships, even where you didn't contribute to it. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md))
- [ ] `BIZ-7` (9) Don't offer, promise or give a bribe, and don't ask for or accept one, whether from public officials or from people in other companies, and don't use agents, consultants, suppliers or joint venture partners to pay one for you. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md)) *Supported by: An Anti-Corruption Ethics and Compliance Programme for Business: A Practical Guide.*

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `BIZ-2` (8) Carry out human rights due diligence across your value chain and keep it going: identify and assess actual and potential harms, act on what you find, check whether your response works, and report how you deal with them. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md)) *Supported by: UN Guiding Principles Reporting Framework.*
- [ ] `BIZ-4` (8) Where you have caused or contributed to harm, provide for or cooperate in putting it right through legitimate processes. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md))
- [ ] `BIZ-5` (8) Never retaliate against workers, their representatives or anyone else who investigates or raises concerns about your operations, and take steps to stop your business partners from doing so. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md))
- [ ] `BIZ-8` (8) Keep complete and accurate books and records: no off-the-books accounts, unrecorded or disguised transactions, made-up expenses or false documents, and don't destroy records before the law allows. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md)) *Supported by: An Anti-Corruption Ethics and Compliance Programme for Business: A Practical Guide.*
- [ ] `BIZ-12` (8) Deal honestly with customers: give them accurate, clear information that lets them make informed choices, make products that are safe in foreseeable use, protect their data, and offer fair, easy ways to complain and get redress. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md))
- [ ] `BIZ-3` (7) If a business partner is causing harm, use your leverage to make it stop before you walk away, and decide whether to end the relationship only after weighing the harm that ending it could itself cause. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md)) *Supported by: Common Framework for Responsible Purchasing Practices.*
- [ ] `BIZ-6` (7) Set up, or take part in, a grievance mechanism that people affected by your operations can use early and directly, and make it legitimate, accessible, predictable, equitable, transparent, compatible with human rights, a source of learning, and based on dialogue. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md))
- [ ] `BIZ-9` (7) Don't evade tax: follow both the letter and the spirit of the tax laws in every country where you operate, pay on time, give the tax authorities the information they need, and set transfer prices at arm's length. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md))
- [ ] `BIZ-10` (7) Don't make facilitation payments, the small payments demanded to speed up routine government actions such as customs clearance or permits, and record accurately any payment you are forced to make to protect someone's life, safety or liberty. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md)) *Supported by: An Anti-Corruption Ethics and Compliance Programme for Business: A Practical Guide.*
- [ ] `BIZ-11` (7) Report honestly to investors and the public: disclose timely, reliable, clear, complete and accurate information on your financial results and on how you deal with your impacts on people and the environment. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md)) *Supported by: UN Guiding Principles Reporting Framework.*
- [ ] `BIZ-13` (7) Deal fairly with suppliers: agree fair payment terms and pay in full and on time, plan orders with them, avoid last-minute changes, and pay prices that cover the cost of decent wages and working conditions. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md)) *Supported by: Common Framework for Responsible Purchasing Practices.*
- [ ] `BIZ-14` (7) Compete fairly: never agree with competitors to fix prices, rig bids, limit output or share out markets, customers or suppliers, and cooperate with competition authorities when they investigate. ([specific/business](../specific/business/README.md))
