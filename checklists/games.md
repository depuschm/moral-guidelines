# Games checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

This file is generated by `python3 scripts/rank.py fix` from the guidelines. Don't edit it by hand.

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `GAME-3` (8) Don't let children buy loot boxes or other paid random items without a parent's or carer's consent, and never urge children to buy or to ask their parents to. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md)) *Supported by: PEGI, Parental control tools.*
- [ ] `GAME-6` (8) If children may play, switch voice and text chat with strangers off by default, use high-privacy settings, and get a parent's consent before collecting a child's data where the law requires it. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-8` (8) As a player, treat other players as you would face to face: no harassment, hate, threats, or cheating and griefing that spoil other people's games. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-1` (7) Tell players before they buy or download a game that it sells paid random items, such as loot boxes, card packs or gacha pulls, and show the odds of each item or category before every purchase, in plain words, close to the point of purchase. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-2` (7) Show the real-money price of everything that players buy with virtual currency, and sell currency in amounts that match what items cost, so that players aren't left with leftovers that push them to buy more. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-4` (7) Ask for clear confirmation before every real-money purchase, give refunds when a child or someone else spent without permission, and never lock the accounts of players who dispute a charge. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-5` (7) Don't build the game's income on a few very high spenders: give every player spending limits and a spending history, and treat extreme spending as a warning sign, not a target. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-7` (7) Give every player easy ways to mute, block and report others, publish clear rules of conduct, and act on reports quickly and consistently. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md)) *Supported by: Planning a Penalty and Reporting System.*
- [ ] `GAME-9` (7) Don't punish players for taking a break: avoid streaks, timers and daily rewards that take away progress or items when people stop playing, and let players pause and save at any point. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-11` (7) Plan accessibility from the game design document onwards, starting with the basics: remappable controls, subtitles for all important speech, no information given by colour or sound alone, a choice of difficulty levels, and playtests with disabled players. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md)) *Supported by: Game Accessibility Guidelines, Full list.*
- [ ] `GAME-12` (7) Plan schedules without crunch: don't treat long hours as a normal part of the job, and pay for overtime or give time off in return. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md))
- [ ] `GAME-10` (6) Support healthy play: show players their playtime, offer reminders and limits, work with the platforms' parental controls, and tell players where to find help if gaming starts to harm their life. ([specific/games](../specific/games/README.md)) *Supported by: PEGI, Parental control tools.*
