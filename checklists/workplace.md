# Workplace and leadership checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

## Fundamental (9–10)

- [ ] `WORK-1` (10) Never use forced or child labour, including work that is exacted under threat of a penalty or not freely chosen, and debt bondage. ([specific/workplace](../specific/workplace/README.md)) *Supported by: ILO indicators of forced labour.*
- [ ] `WORK-2` (9) Recruit, pay, train, promote and dismiss people on the basis of their ability to do the job, never because of race, colour, sex, religion, political opinion, national extraction or social origin, or other grounds that the law protects where you work, such as age, disability or sexual orientation. ([specific/workplace](../specific/workplace/README.md))
- [ ] `WORK-3` (9) Pay women and men equally for work of equal value, not only for the same work: compare jobs on objective factors, such as the qualifications, effort and responsibility they need and the conditions they are done in, not on who usually does them. ([specific/workplace](../specific/workplace/README.md)) *Supported by: Promoting Equity: gender-neutral job evaluation for equal pay. A step-by-step guide.*
- [ ] `WORK-6` (9) Make work safe and healthy: keep workplaces, machinery, equipment and processes safe so far as reasonably practicable, consult workers on safety, don't make them pay for safety measures, and never penalise someone for leaving a situation they reasonably believe is an imminent and serious danger. ([specific/workplace](../specific/workplace/README.md)) *Supported by: Guidelines on occupational safety and health management systems (ILO-OSH 2001).*
- [ ] `WORK-7` (9) Don't harass anyone and don't tolerate violence or harassment at work, including sexual and other gender-based harassment: adopt a policy with your workers, assess the risks, train people, and offer safe, fair ways to complain. ([specific/workplace](../specific/workplace/README.md))
- [ ] `WORK-9` (9) Never retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith about wrongdoing, danger or harassment, whether they raise it inside the organisation or with a regulator, and protect their identity. ([specific/workplace](../specific/workplace/README.md)) *Supported by: Whistleblowing guidance for employers.*
