# Data and analytics checklist

Copy this list into a product or design review, a project kickoff, a team charter or your own reflection, and tick what applies.
Rules are grouped by level and sorted by score ([criteria](../RANKING.md)). Each links to the file that explains it and cites its sources.
*Supported by* names practices and tools that help you follow the rule (see the file's "How to apply" section).

Also apply the [general checklist](general.md).

This file is generated by `python3 scripts/rank.py fix` from the guidelines. Don't edit it by hand.

## Recommended (6–8)

- [ ] `DATA-1` (8) Before you collect or reuse data about people, state a specific, legitimate purpose, and don't use the data for a new purpose that is incompatible with it. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md)) *Supported by: Data and AI Ethics Self-Assessment Tool, Data Protection Impact Assessments.*
- [ ] `DATA-2` (8) Collect and keep only the data your purpose needs, for no longer than it needs it, and use anonymised or synthetic data where that will do. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md)) *Supported by: Data Protection Impact Assessments.*
- [ ] `DATA-5` (8) Where you rely on consent, make it a real choice, freely given, specific and informed, let people withdraw it as easily as they gave it, and use their data only as they agreed. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md))
- [ ] `DATA-7` (8) Don't treat data as anonymous until you have tested whether people could be identified from it, alone or linked with other data, and treat pseudonymised data as personal data. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md)) *Supported by: Motivated intruder test.*
- [ ] `DATA-8` (8) Don't try to re-identify people in data that was shared or published in de-identified form unless whoever de-identified it has agreed, for example to test how well it was done; report a weakness you find to them instead. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md)) *Supported by: Motivated intruder test.*
- [ ] `DATA-9` (8) Protect the data you hold against unauthorised access, loss and misuse, limit who can see it, and plan how you will respond to a breach. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md)) *Supported by: Data Protection Impact Assessments.*
- [ ] `DATA-3` (7) Check that your data is accurate, up to date and fit for your purpose, and document where it came from, how it was collected and processed, and its gaps and limits. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md))
- [ ] `DATA-4` (7) Tell people, in plain words and when you collect their data, what you collect, why, who will receive it, how long you will keep it and what rights they have. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md))
- [ ] `DATA-6` (7) Let people find out what data you hold about them, get a copy, and have it corrected or deleted. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md))
- [ ] `DATA-10` (7) Check data for bias before you rely on it: whether it represents the people it will be used about, and whether some variables act as proxies for protected characteristics, as a postcode can for age or race. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md)) *Supported by: Data and AI Ethics Self-Assessment Tool.*
- [ ] `DATA-11` (7) Make sure the people the data is about share in its benefits, and involve them in decisions about how it is used, especially communities with collective rights and interests in data, such as Indigenous Peoples. ([specific/data](../specific/data/README.md)) *Supported by: Data and AI Ethics Self-Assessment Tool.*
