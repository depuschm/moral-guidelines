# Reading list

Complete courses, reference works, open textbooks and classic texts, referenced as a whole. Topic files link to specific chapters and entries where relevant. For what each religious and philosophical tradition teaches, start from the [tradition pages](../traditions/README.md), which link to the traditions' own texts.

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Free online courses, guides and reference works

- `8` [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/): the leading free scholarly reference work in philosophy, with peer-reviewed, regularly revised entries on every major moral theory, concept and debate.
- `7` [Justice](https://justiceharvard.org/) (Michael Sandel, Harvard University): a free course of recorded lectures that works through moral questions, such as markets, rights and justice, with the theories of Aristotle, Kant, Mill and Rawls.
- `7` [Introduction to Philosophy: Ethics](https://press.rebus.community/intro-to-phil-ethics/) (Rebus Community, 2019): a free, openly licensed (CC BY) textbook covering metaethics, the main normative theories and applied ethics.
- `6` [Ethics Unwrapped](https://ethicsunwrapped.utexas.edu/) (University of Texas at Austin): short videos and case studies on behavioural ethics, ethics concepts and real cases, free for teaching.
- `6` [Ethics in Technology Practice](https://www.scu.edu/ethics-in-technology-practice/) (Markkula Center for Applied Ethics): a free toolkit and workshop materials for building ethical practice into technology teams.
- `4` [BBC Ethics guide](https://www.bbc.co.uk/ethics/introduction/) (BBC, archived and no longer updated): plain-language introductions to ethical theories and to the views of religions on ethical questions.

## Classic texts, free online

- `8` [Nicomachean Ethics](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.html) (Aristotle, translated by W. D. Ross, Internet Classics Archive): the founding work of virtue ethics, on happiness, the virtues, practical wisdom, justice and friendship.
