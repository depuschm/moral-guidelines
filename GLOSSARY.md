# Glossary

The abbreviations used in the guidelines, with their meaning and the guideline where they matter most. On the [website](https://depuschm.github.io/moral-guidelines/), each of them shows its meaning when you hover over it. Everyday terms, such as AI, EU, UK, UN or US, aren't listed, and neither are abbreviations that mean different things in different fields, such as AMA or ASA.

When a guideline introduces an abbreviation that readers may not know, add a row here, in alphabetical order. Keep the meaning to one plain sentence without links or formatting, because the website uses it as the tooltip text.
