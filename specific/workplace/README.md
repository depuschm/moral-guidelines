# Workplace and leadership

Scope: employers, managers and anyone who leads others at work, in companies, public bodies and non-profit organisations: fair recruitment and pay, non-discrimination, safety, freedom from harassment, freedom of association, speaking up, credit for work, and leaders' example. The general guidelines on [fairness and justice](../../general/fairness.md), [human rights](../../general/human-rights.md) and [responsibility and accountability](../../general/responsibility.md) also apply. Labour rights in suppliers and the rest of the value chain are covered in [Business and human rights](../business/README.md), and the duties of public servants in [Public service](../public-service/README.md). This is moral guidance, not legal advice: employment law differs between countries, so check what applies to you with an employment lawyer or your labour inspectorate.

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Adopted references

- `9` [ILO Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work](https://www.ilo.org/resource/conference-paper/ilo-1998-declaration-fundamental-principles-and-rights-work-and-its-follow) (International Labour Organization, 1998, amended in 2022): **the baseline**: the five principles that every ILO member must respect, whether or not it has ratified the conventions behind them: freedom of association and collective bargaining, no forced labour, no child labour, no discrimination in employment, and a safe and healthy working environment.
- `8` [Violence and Harassment Convention, 2019 (No. 190)](https://www.ilo.org/resource/ilc/ilc108/violence-and-harassment-convention-2019-no-190) (ILO): the international convention on violence and harassment in the world of work, including gender-based violence and sexual harassment, with the steps employers should take to prevent them.
- `8` [Equal Remuneration Convention, 1951 (No. 100)](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%20165/volume-165-I-2181-English.pdf) (ILO, text in the UN Treaty Series): equal pay for men and women for work of equal value, assessed by objective appraisal of the jobs.
- `7` [Protection for whistleblowers](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/human-rights/your-fundamental-rights-eu/protection-whistleblowers_en) (European Commission): the EU Whistleblower Protection Directive (EU) 2019/1937, which requires confidential internal and external reporting channels, follow-up of reports and protection from retaliation for people who report breaches of EU law.
- `7` [General principles and operational guidelines for fair recruitment](https://www.ilo.org/publications/general-principles-and-operational-guidelines-fair-recruitment-and) (ILO, 2019): principles for recruiting workers, including migrant workers, without fees, deception or coercion, with the ILO's definition of recruitment fees and related costs.

## Key rules

### Fair recruitment and pay

- `10` `WORK-1` **Never use forced or child labour, including work that is exacted under threat of a penalty or not freely chosen, and debt bondage.** Watch for the warning signs in your own workplace and in the agencies you use, such as withheld wages or identity documents, restricted movement, debts to recruiters and excessive overtime. ([ILO Declaration, paragraph 2](https://www.ilo.org/resource/conference-paper/ilo-1998-declaration-fundamental-principles-and-rights-work-and-its-follow), [ILO indicators of forced labour](https://www.ilo.org/publications/ilo-indicators-forced-labour)) See `RIGHTS-2`.
- `9` `WORK-2` **Recruit, pay, train, promote and dismiss people on the basis of their ability to do the job, never because of race, colour, sex, religion, political opinion, national extraction or social origin, or other grounds that the law protects where you work, such as age, disability or sexual orientation.** ([ILO Discrimination (Employment and Occupation) Convention, 1958 (No. 111), Article 1](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%20362/volume-362-I-5181-English.pdf), [ILO Declaration, paragraph 2(d)](https://www.ilo.org/resource/conference-paper/ilo-1998-declaration-fundamental-principles-and-rights-work-and-its-follow)) See `FAIR-1`.
- `9` `WORK-3` **Pay women and men equally for work of equal value, not only for the same work: compare jobs on objective factors, such as the qualifications, effort and responsibility they need and the conditions they are done in, not on who usually does them.** ([ILO Convention No. 100, Articles 1 to 3](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%20165/volume-165-I-2181-English.pdf), [ILO, Promoting Equity: gender-neutral job evaluation for equal pay](https://www.ilo.org/publications/promoting-equity-gender-neutral-job-evaluation-equal-pay-step-step-guide))
- `8` `WORK-4` **Recruit fairly: don't charge workers or jobseekers any recruitment fees or related costs, give them clear terms they can understand, preferably in a written contract, and never confiscate or keep their identity documents.** The same principles apply to the recruitment agencies you use. ([ILO fair recruitment principles 7, 8 and 11](https://www.ilo.org/publications/general-principles-and-operational-guidelines-fair-recruitment-and))
- `8` `WORK-5` **Pay fair wages that allow a decent life, and respect reasonable limits on working hours, rest and paid holidays.** ([Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Articles 23 and 24](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights))

### Safety, dignity and freedom of association

- `9` `WORK-6` **Make work safe and healthy: keep workplaces, machinery, equipment and processes safe so far as reasonably practicable, consult workers on safety, don't make them pay for safety measures, and never penalise someone for leaving a situation they reasonably believe is an imminent and serious danger.** ([ILO Declaration, paragraph 2(e)](https://www.ilo.org/resource/conference-paper/ilo-1998-declaration-fundamental-principles-and-rights-work-and-its-follow), [ILO Occupational Safety and Health Convention, 1981 (No. 155), Articles 13, 16, 19 and 21](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%201331/volume-1331-I-22345-English.pdf)) See `HARM-4`.
- `9` `WORK-7` **Don't harass anyone and don't tolerate violence or harassment at work, including sexual and other gender-based harassment: adopt a policy with your workers, assess the risks, train people, and offer safe, fair ways to complain.** ([ILO Convention No. 190, Articles 1, 9 and 10](https://www.ilo.org/resource/ilc/ilc108/violence-and-harassment-convention-2019-no-190)) See `RESP-1`.
- `8` `WORK-8` **Respect workers' freedom to form and join trade unions and to bargain collectively, and never refuse to hire, dismiss or otherwise punish anyone for union membership or activities.** ([ILO Declaration, paragraph 2(a)](https://www.ilo.org/resource/conference-paper/ilo-1998-declaration-fundamental-principles-and-rights-work-and-its-follow), [ILO Right to Organise and Collective Bargaining Convention, 1949 (No. 98), Article 1](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%2096/volume-96-I-1341-English.pdf))

### Speaking up

- `9` `WORK-9` **Never retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith about wrongdoing, danger or harassment, whether they raise it inside the organisation or with a regulator, and protect their identity.** Retaliation includes threats and attempts as well as acts such as dismissal, cutting someone's hours or harassing them. ([European Commission, Report on the Whistleblower Protection Directive, Article 19](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/7cc63350-88c9-4c0b-a46e-04fc11e673e7_en), [UK government, Whistleblowing guidance for employers](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/whistleblowing-guidance-for-employers)) See `ACCT-10`.
- `7` `WORK-10` **Give people safe ways to raise concerns in confidence, and anonymously if they wish; investigate what they report independently and in proportion, and act on it.** In the EU, private employers with 50 or more workers must have internal reporting channels. ([UK Corporate Governance Code 2024, Provision 6](https://www.frc.org.uk/documents/6709/UK_Corporate_Governance_Code_2024_a2hmQmY.pdf), [European Commission, Protection for whistleblowers](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/human-rights/your-fundamental-rights-eu/protection-whistleblowers_en), [Report on the Whistleblower Protection Directive, Article 8(3)](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/7cc63350-88c9-4c0b-a46e-04fc11e673e7_en))

### Leadership and credit

- `7` `WORK-11` **Lead by example: if you lead others, act with integrity yourself, show in your own conduct the behaviour you ask of them, and promote a culture of integrity.** ([UK Corporate Governance Code 2024, Principle B](https://www.frc.org.uk/documents/6709/UK_Corporate_Governance_Code_2024_a2hmQmY.pdf), [Council of Europe Model Code of Conduct for Public Officials, Article 25](https://www.jus.uio.no/english/services/library/treaties/04/4-04/public-officials.html))
- `6` `WORK-12` **Give credit for work to the people who did it, in reports, presentations and appraisals, and don't present a colleague's or a team member's work as your own.** ([US National Society of Professional Engineers, Code of Ethics for Engineers, III.9](https://www.nspe.org/sites/default/files/resources/pdfs/Ethics/CodeofEthics/NSPECodeofEthicsforEngineers.pdf), [American Marketing Association, Statement of Ethics: Transparency](https://www.ama.org/ama-statement-of-ethics/)) See `HON-9`.

## How to apply

- `7` [Promoting Equity: gender-neutral job evaluation for equal pay. A step-by-step guide](https://www.ilo.org/publications/promoting-equity-gender-neutral-job-evaluation-equal-pay-step-step-guide) (ILO, 2008): how to compare jobs on qualifications, effort, responsibility and working conditions, and how to estimate pay gaps between jobs of equal value and adjust pay. Supports `WORK-3`.
- `7` [Guidelines on occupational safety and health management systems (ILO-OSH 2001)](https://www.ilo.org/publications/guidelines-occupational-safety-and-health-management-systems-ilo-osh-2001) (ILO): how an organisation can build occupational safety and health into its overall policies and management arrangements. Supports `WORK-6`.
- `6` [Whistleblowing guidance for employers](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/whistleblowing-guidance-for-employers) (UK government): what whistleblowing law protects in Great Britain, how to handle a disclosure, and what a good whistleblowing policy contains. Supports `WORK-9` and `WORK-10`.
- `6` [ILO indicators of forced labour](https://www.ilo.org/publications/ilo-indicators-forced-labour): eleven warning signs of forced labour, such as abuse of vulnerability, retention of identity documents, withholding of wages and debt bondage, with real-life examples. Supports `WORK-1` and `WORK-4`.

## Open questions

Questions on which serious traditions or experts disagree. These guidelines don't pick a side.

- `6` [Make Work Pay: workplace monitoring technologies](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/make-work-pay-workplace-monitoring-technologies) (UK government consultation, 2026): how far may employers monitor their workers? Monitoring can improve security, compliance, safety and the tracking of performance, but it also risks privacy and autonomy, disproportionate surveillance, unfair decisions based on poor data and a loss of trust; people disagree about where the line lies and how much say workers should have.
- `6` [The role of digital labour platforms in transforming the world of work](https://www.ilo.org/publications/flagship-reports/role-digital-labour-platforms-transforming-world-work) (ILO World Employment and Social Outlook, 2021): should people who find work through apps be treated as employees or as self-employed? Many workers value the flexibility, but pay is often low, most lack social security, and algorithms manage their work; countries range from treating them as employees, because of the platform's control, to treating them as independent contractors, because of their flexibility and autonomy, with intermediate categories in between. The ILO's [Decent Work in the Platform Economy Convention, 2026 (No. 193)](https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/how-will-new-convention-no193-promote-decent-work-platform-economy) sets a floor of rights for platform workers whatever their status; it doesn't impose a classification, but requires each country to make sure that status is determined correctly, mainly from the facts of the work.

## Tutorials and further reading

- `7` [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Business Ethics: 6. The firm and its workers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-business/#FirmWork): the ethics of hiring and firing, pay, meaningful work and whistleblowing.
- `6` [Equal Pay: An introductory guide](https://www.ilo.org/publications/equal-pay-introductory-guide-0) (ILO, 2013): the concepts behind equal pay for work of equal value, and how to apply the principle in practice.
- `5` [Rules of the Game: An introduction to the standards-related work of the International Labour Organization](https://www.ilo.org/publications/rules-game-introduction-standards-related-work-international-labour) (ILO, centenary edition, 2019): an introduction to international labour standards and how the ILO supervises them, with developments up to early 2019. It predates the Violence and Harassment Convention and the 2022 amendment of the Declaration.
