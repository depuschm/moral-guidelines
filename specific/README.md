# Domain guidelines

One folder per profession, field or area of life. Each folder adopts the field's own code of ethics and the international instruments that govern it where they exist, and adds short, sourced rules only where needed. The [general guidelines](../general/README.md) still apply.

### Technology

- [Software engineering](software/README.md): the duties of people who build software, from the ACM Code of Ethics: the public good, quality, honesty about what systems can do, and refusing harmful work.
- [Artificial intelligence](ai/README.md): fairness, transparency, human oversight, safety and accountability for AI systems, from the UNESCO and OECD principles and the EU AI Act.
- [Data and analytics](data/README.md): collecting, sharing and analysing data about people responsibly: purpose, consent, minimisation, bias and transparency.
- [Product and UX design](design/README.md): designing products that respect users: no deceptive patterns, accessibility, attention and wellbeing.
- [Games](games/README.md): monetisation and loot boxes, children, player wellbeing, toxic play and the people who make games.
- [Security research](security/README.md): finding and disclosing vulnerabilities responsibly, and the limits of hacking for good.

### Business and work

- [Business and human rights](business/README.md): companies' responsibility to respect human rights, avoid corruption and deal fairly, from the UN Guiding Principles and the OECD Guidelines.
- [Marketing and advertising](marketing/README.md): honest advertising, disclosed endorsements, and marketing to children and vulnerable people.
- [Workplace and leadership](workplace/README.md): fair treatment at work, fundamental labour rights, speaking up and ethical leadership.

### Science and health

- [Research with people and animals](research/README.md): informed consent, risks and benefits, fair selection, ethics review and research integrity.
- [Healthcare](healthcare/README.md): the duties of health professionals to patients: their wellbeing, autonomy, confidentiality and fair access.

### Media and public life

- [Journalism](journalism/README.md): seeking the truth, minimising harm, independence and accountability in news.
- [Public service](public-service/README.md): integrity, impartiality and accountability for people who hold public office or work for the state.
- [Online life and social media](online/README.md): how individuals treat others online: sharing, misinformation, harassment and other people's images and data.

## Adding a domain

Copy [`_template/`](_template/README.md) to a new lower-case folder and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#5-adding-a-domain-or-a-tradition).
